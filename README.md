### 🌌 Exoplanet Discovery Analysis
> *Reverse-engineering how humanity detects worlds beyond our solar system using 5,000+ NASA-verified records.*

```
Dataset  →  5,000+ NASA Confirmed Exoplanets
Stack    →  Python (Pandas, Seaborn) · Looker Studio
Key Find →  Identified the "Kepler Effect": Transit Method accounts for
            71%+ of all verified detections — a methodological dominance
            invisible without longitudinal analysis.
```

**What made it interesting:** Not just charting discoveries by year — but mapping *how* discovery methods evolved and which telescope missions caused phase shifts in the data distribution.

### 🛵 Zomato Last-Mile Delivery Analytics
> *Pinpointing the real bottlenecks in hyperlocal food logistics — beyond the obvious.*

| Finding | Insight |
|---|---|
| **Multi-order batching** | Increases average wait time by **+80%** — bigger impact than traffic |
| **Peak efficiency window** | 4 PM – 7 PM in Metro hubs shows lowest delay-to-order ratio |
| **Spatial analysis** | Heatmaps reveal delivery density ≠ delivery efficiency |

> *The counter-intuitive result: rush-hour congestion was not the primary villain. Operational decisions—specifically route bundling—drove the largest delays.*
